Liqiang Xiao

Senior Applied Scientist · Amazon

I obtained my PhD from Shanghai Jiao Tong University and completed my bachelor's degree in Information Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University. From 2018 to 2019, I visited the Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University, where I was mentored by Professor Lu Wang.

My research is focused on LLM alignment, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. More specifically, my work is mostly about text summarization, conversational search, multi-task learning, as well as the application of reinforcement learning on NLP.
